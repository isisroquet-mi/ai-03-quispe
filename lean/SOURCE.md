# Source record

- Paper: Alexander Quispe and Kunmeng Xu (2026), *Coding Beyond Your Training: Claude Code and the Technological Frontier of Software Developers*.
- Canonical record: <https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.25438>
- Source used for this pass: the threshold model and derivation reproduced in the repository `README.md`, checked against the paper record.
- Selected scope: the solo and delegation thresholds, their algebraic difference, the minimum-threshold menu result, the activation band, and the monotone-CDF implication for expected language expansion.

This pass does not claim to formalize the empirical design or to prove that Claude Code causally lowers a threshold. It proves the mathematical implications conditional on the model definitions and visible premises.
